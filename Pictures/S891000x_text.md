## S8910001.jpg — p.3
**Japanese translation of Rob's haiku.**
by Yoshinori kawagita
*(dated 2026-08-08)*

1. Brown littele dog—
Bringing me its favorite toy,
dreaming of stick.

茶毛《ちゃげ》の犬　おもちゃ運《はこ》びて　棒夢見《ぼうゆめみ》る

↓

夢《ゆめ》の中《なか》　仔犬《こいぬ》おもちゃを　咥《くわ》えくる

Dreaming of a pup,
running toward me right now
holding its tight toy.

※ Let's leave it to the reader's imagination what kind of toy it is.

Expressions using season words.
夏《なつ》の夢《ゆめ》　仔犬《こいぬ》おもちゃを　咥《くわ》えくる

2. Within the tree rings,
the threads of living beings
weave another spring.

年輪《ねんりん》に　命《いのち》の糸《いと》は　春《はる》を編《あ》む
(As it is.)

or

年輪《ねんりん》や　命《いのち》の糸《いと》は　春《はる》を編《あ》む

by Yoshinori kawagita
*(dated 2026-08-08, "10/2")*

The waterfall of time—
I step beneath its silver veil:
yesterday drifts away.
