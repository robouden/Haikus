# Father Haiku

灰色の霧   
金剛の心に   
笑みひらく   

*Haiiro no kiri / kongō no kokoro ni / emi hiraku*   
Here, **金剛 (kongō)** means *diamond* in the sense of something **adamantine, indestructible, and spiritually pure**, rather than the gemstone itself. This gives the poem a deeper Zen/Buddhist resonance. I think this version would feel the most natural and evocative to a Japanese reader if you're aiming for a contemplative haiku.
