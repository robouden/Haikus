### A small refinement

There is one line I'd be tempted to polish even further:

> **森《もり》の弦《げん》**

("the forest's strings")

It is elegant, but perhaps a bit compressed. Since your English says *"cicada voices flow like violins through the forest,"* I rather like:

> 蝉時雨《せみしぐれ》
> 森《もり》に弦《げん》の音《ね》
> 流《なが》れゆく

or

> 蝉時雨《せみしぐれ》
> 森《もり》に弦《げん》の調《しら》べ
> 流《なが》るる

These evoke the sound of violin strings without explicitly saying "violin," making the image feel more Japanese while preserving the musical metaphor.

I think this renku is becoming something quite special: it moves from **sharing**, to **the living music of the mountain**, to **the quiet expectation of a friend's return**—a progression that feels both personal and deeply rooted in the landscape.
