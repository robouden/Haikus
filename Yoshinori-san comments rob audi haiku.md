# These are my comments on Rob's haiku.

**by Yoshinori kawagita**

### 1.

**山の夜　眠る松の上　星またたく**
 *(As it is.)*

Quiet mountain night. Stars blink above sleeping pines.

↓

**松深く　眠る山の夜　星涼し**
 *(I used a seasonal word.)*

Deep among the pines, the mountain sleeps in the night,
 stars so cool and clear.

------

### 2.

**黒きアウディ　星の下にて　熱冷ます**
 *(As it is.)*

A black Audi waits, cooling beneath the stars.

※ stars or summer stars

------

### 3.

**肩あらわ　本の頁を　そっと繰る**
 *(As it is. Bare shoulders emphasize a summer vibe in this shot.)*

Bare shoulders, a book another page quietly turns.

------

### 4.

Original:

**月をまとう絹　ひと声の悲鳴　夜を裂く**

↓

Revised:

**月光をまとう絹　悲鳴一声　静寂裂く**

*(I changed the moon into moonlight.
 The moonlight tells me it's night.)*

Silk wearing moonlight. One sudden scream break the silence.

------

### 5.

**山さえも　息をひそめる　一瞬に**
 *(As it is.)*

Even the mountain holds its breath for one moment.

*(I'd prefer to use seasonal word if possible.)*

------

### 6.

Original:

**星は変わらず　またたく**
 *(Let's use the 5-7-5 structure.)*

↓

**きらきらと　星は変わらず　瞬けり**

Twinkling bright, the stars shine on unchanged.

------

Alternative:

**山里の　空を余さず　星涼し**

Mountain village sky, Filled completely with bright stars.
 Bringing a cool night.
